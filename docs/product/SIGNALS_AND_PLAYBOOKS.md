# Signals and Playbooks

Signals and playbooks are structured research artifacts. They are not trading instructions, investment advice, or promises of future returns.

Required disclaimer: `This is not financial advice.`

## Signals

Current signals are generated by `apps/api/services/signal_service.py` from mock research data. Each signal includes:

- Asset.
- Signal type.
- Direction such as `long_watch` or `neutral`.
- Confidence score.
- Risk score.
- Thesis.
- Catalyst.
- Invalidation.
- Timeframe.

API:

```bash
curl -X POST http://localhost:8000/signals/scan \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN"
```

The scan costs 8 credits through `sentiment_scan`.

## Playbooks

Playbooks are generated from strategy classes in `packages/strategies`.

Current strategy list:

- BTC momentum breakout.
- ETH/BTC rotation.
- SOL high beta rotation.
- HYPE trend following.
- MSTR premium / BTC proxy trade.
- STRC event-driven credit trade.

Generate:

```bash
curl -X POST http://localhost:8000/playbooks/generate \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN"
```

The action costs 30 credits and requires high-cost task entitlement.

## Playbook Fields

Each playbook should include:

- Strategy name.
- Asset.
- Thesis.
- Trigger.
- Entry condition.
- Exit condition.
- Invalidation.
- Risk score.
- Confidence.
- Timeframe.
- Expected payoff.
- Required data sources.

## Interpretation Rules

- A signal direction is a research label, not an order side.
- Confidence is an internal quality score, not probability of profit.
- Risk score is relative and source-dependent.
- Invalidation is a condition to revisit the thesis, not automated execution.
- Backtests and playbooks must not be marketed as future-performance evidence.
