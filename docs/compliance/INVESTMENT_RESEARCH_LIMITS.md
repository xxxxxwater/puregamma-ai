# Investment Research Limits
PureGamma AI is an investment research product, not an adviser, broker, custodian, tax tool, or execution system.
## Allowed
- Market summaries.
- Portfolio-aware research context.
- Signals with thesis, catalyst, invalidation, and risk.
- Strategy playbooks.
- Hypothetical backtests.
- Notification delivery of research.
- NAV estimates with source freshness.
## Not Allowed
- Personalized investment advice.
- Guaranteed return claims.
- Order placement.
- Automated execution.
- Custody of funds.
- Seed phrase or private key handling.
- Tax filing or official statements.
## Language Guidance
Use:
- "Research watchlist."
- "Review condition."
- "Potential risk."
- "Estimated NAV."
- "Hypothetical backtest."
Avoid:
- "Buy now."
- "Sell now."
- "Guaranteed."
- "Risk-free."
- "Official account value."
## Enterprise Notes
Enterprise customers may require additional review for:
- Archiving.
- Data retention.
- Supervisory review.
- Approved disclaimers.
- Data licensing restrictions.
